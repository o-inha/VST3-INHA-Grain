INHA Grate 0.5.1 - Windows x64 VST3

1. Close your DAW.
2. Copy the entire "INHA Grate.vst3" folder to:
   C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
3. Put your manually generated inha-licence.key in:
   INHA Grate.vst3\Contents\Resources\
4. Rescan VST3 plug-ins in your DAW and insert INHA Grate on an audio or
   instrument track. For MIDI NOTES mode, route MIDI notes to this effect
   using the routing provided by your DAW.

Keep the whole .vst3 folder together. Do not copy just the binary inside it.
The ZIP contains no licence key. Product identifier: inha-grate.

Drag controls vertically; Shift gives fine adjustment. For ranges, Ctrl moves
both ends together and Alt changes the width. Double-click resets a control.
Use the gear in the footer for light/dark theme and 100%/150% size.

Read README.md for capture, UNDULATION/SWAY and repeatability behavior.
Saved 0.1/0.2 settings migrate duration values into the new limits.
Each motion tab retains its own AMOUNT/SPEED settings. NONE uses SWAY.

Free durations: 40 MS to 6 S. SYNC: 1/16 note to 2 bars (4/4).
MASS selects 1x to 8x wet layers with progressively lower gain.
Added layers use their own repeatable slow SWAY at 5-25 percent. PAN varies each grain's stereo balance.
BUFFER caps the effective GRAIN size; pink values identify requested ranges
that currently exceed BUFFER. Raising BUFFER restores those requested sizes.

In SYNC mode, T beside BUFFER and GRAIN independently adds triplet divisions.

The renamed product uses a new VST3 identity and the licence product
inha-grate. Existing instances of the previous product remain separate.
