INHA Grain 0.2.0 - Windows x64 VST3

1. Close your DAW.
2. Copy the entire "INHA Grain.vst3" folder to:
   C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
3. Put your manually generated inha-licence.key in:
   INHA Grain.vst3\Contents\Resources\
4. Rescan VST3 plug-ins in your DAW and insert INHA Grain on an audio or
   instrument track. For MIDI NOTES mode, route MIDI notes to this effect
   using the routing provided by your DAW.

Keep the whole .vst3 folder together. Do not copy just the binary inside it.
The ZIP contains no licence key. Product identifier: inha-grain.

Drag controls vertically; Shift gives fine adjustment. For ranges, Ctrl moves
both ends together and Alt changes the width. Double-click resets a control.
Use the gear in the footer for light/dark theme and 100%/150% size.

Read README.md for capture, UNDULATION/SWAY and repeatability behavior.
Existing 0.1.0 project settings load in UNDULATION mode with their original
parameters. Each motion tab retains its own AMOUNT/SPEED settings.
